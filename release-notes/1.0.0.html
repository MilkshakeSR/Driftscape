Driftscape 1.0.0

First release

Added

  • Live wallpapers on every display and every Space, drawn behind your desktop icons with Metal, including behind the menu bar and notch.
  • 38 original, procedurally generated wallpapers in six collections: Water, Rain & Windows, Space, Nature, City & Cosy and Abstract.
  • Real time of day: the sun's position for your location drives sunrise colours, golden hour, dusk and starry nights, with the moon in its real phase.
  • Real weather from Open-Meteo every 30 minutes: clouds, rain, snow, fog, wind and thunderstorms fade in smoothly (rain takes two minutes to arrive). A weather preview menu lets you try Clear, Cloudy, Rain, Storm, Snow and Fog.
  • Optional music reactivity (off by default): wallpapers breathe gently with what your Mac is playing, using system audio, screen-capture audio or an input device. Sound is analysed live and never recorded.
  • Library with live hover previews, collections, search and favourites.
  • Studio with a large live preview, automatic controls for every setting, time/weather/music toggles, Randomize, Reset, undo and redo, and Save as Preset.
  • Schedules by time of day (dawn, sunrise, day, golden hour, sunset, night or set times) or a shuffle playlist, with crossfades.
  • A different wallpaper for each display.
  • Export a 5K still (PNG) or a seamless 10-second loop (MP4).
  • Lock screen and Mission Control kept in step with a still frame every 15 minutes, with your own wallpaper restored when you quit.
  • A Driftscape screen saver, installed with one click from Settings.
  • Energy saving: 30 fps at 75% resolution by default, and a complete pause when the desktop is covered, the screen is locked or asleep, in Low Power Mode or (optionally) on battery. A live CPU and GPU readout in Settings.
  • Menu bar menu, onboarding, Settings, automatic updates and a screen saver.
